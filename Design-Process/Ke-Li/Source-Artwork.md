# Original Artwork Archive

The 19 files below are copied without re-exporting, resizing, or renaming them. They document artwork contributions only. They are not wired into the app's customization interface.

## Duplicate handling and provenance

The [artwork manifest](Artwork-Manifest.json) maps all 65 original PNGs to their repository locations and records source and repository SHA-256 hashes.

- The 42 matching character files are referenced at their existing locations.
- `image 47.png` maps to `room_blush`, `image 53.png` to `room_gaming`, `image 54.png` to `room_vintage`, and `image 61.png` to `room_sky`.
- Those four room source files differ from the existing PNG bytes, but their dimensions and premultiplied RGBA pixels match. They also render identically against black and white backgrounds. They are referenced instead of uploaded again.
- The remaining 19 files are stored in `Assets/`. Their SHA-256 hashes match the original source files.

This archive does not assign dates, an iteration sequence, or UI design work to these images. `StudySpots_Character_Integrated` is treated as an older project reference, not as new UI work.
