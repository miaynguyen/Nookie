# Seohee Kim — UI/UX and Mid-Fidelity Contribution

This folder documents my contributions to the Nookie midterm project.

## My Role

My primary role was UI/UX design and mid-fidelity SwiftUI development. I helped establish the overall visual direction and user flow of the app before the final development stage.

## Design Contributions

- Developed the Nookie brand direction, logo, color palette, and typography
- Designed the onboarding and sign-up experience
- Designed the home and character study room screens
- Designed the study timer interface
- Designed the map, saved locations, and study-space information cards
- Designed the character and room customization experience
- Designed the profile and account screens
- Created and selected character, clothing, background, and interface assets
- Revised the interface based on team feedback and usability testing

## Development Contributions

I created a mid-fidelity SwiftUI prototype that connected the main app pages and established the screen layouts, navigation structure, buttons, image assets, and visual styling. This version was combined with the team's work and further developed into the final prototype.

During the final iteration, I also contributed feedback and design revisions for the sign-up flow, onboarding, timer, customization system, map information cards, community update flow, and account screens.
